Our Philosophy

We cook over fire because it forces honesty — no shortcuts, no over-engineering. Just good ingredients, close attention, and the patience to let them speak.

In the Kitchen

Every plate starts with the fire.

Our kitchen is built around a single wood-fired hearth — every protein, most vegetables, even some of our desserts pass through it. It's a slower way to cook, and it shows: char where it belongs, restraint everywhere else.

"We're not chasing trends. We're chasing the best version of a few things, cooked properly."

— Executive Chef, Ember House
